## Required Assets for Full SEO

### 1. Create Open Graph Image

Create `/client/public/og-image.jpeg` (1200x630px) with:

- Your domain "onlinemcpinspector.com"
- Screenshot of the tool in action
- Text overlay: "Free Online MCP Inspector"
- Branded design that stands out in social feeds

### 2. Favicon Set

Convert your existing `mcp.svg` to:

```
/favicon.ico (32x32)
/apple-touch-icon.png (180x180)
/favicon-16x16.png
/favicon-32x32.png
/android-chrome-192x192.png
/android-chrome-512x512.png
```

### 3. Google Services Setup

1. **Google Analytics**: Get your `GA_MEASUREMENT_ID`
2. **Google Ads**: Get your `GOOGLE_ADS_CLIENT_ID`
3. **Google Search Console**: Verify your domain
4. **Google Tag Manager** (optional): For advanced tracking
